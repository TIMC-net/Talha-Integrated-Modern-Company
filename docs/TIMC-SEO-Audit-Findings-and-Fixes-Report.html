SEO Audit Report

TIMC — Talha Integrated Modern Company

Full technical, on-page, content, and local SEO audit follow-up for www.trsco.net — a Jeddah-based general contractor and heavy equipment rental company serving projects across Saudi Arabia.

Site: https://www.trsco.net
Source audit: TIMCSEOAudit.pdf (20 August 2026)
Implementation follow-up: 21 August 2026
Prepared by: WeRQA

Executive summary. The independent audit found a solid technical build (clean URLs, sitemap, robots, HTTPS, structured data) but weak commercial visibility — mainly from an unclaimed Google Business Profile, a www/non-www split, thin secondary titles, a truncated equipment-rental meta description, and local/off-page gaps. WeRQA has now implemented the developer-owned fixes from that audit. Items that require TIMC marketing/ops (GBP claim, reviews, Arabic site) remain open and are listed clearly below.

06 — Client takeaway

Before this fix pass

  • Broken/truncated service snippet risk
  • Thin secondary titles
  • Empty schema sameAs

After this fix pass
